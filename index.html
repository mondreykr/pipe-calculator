Footnotes
Basis
t = PD / (2(SE + PY)) (ASME B31.3 para. 304.1.2, eq. 3a) tm = t + CA (eq. 2) tLMC = tNOM × (1 − MT) − CA tNOM,min = tm / (1 − MT) A schedule passes when tLMC ≥ t, which is the same test as tNOM ≥ tNOM,min. EXCESS = tLMC − t.
Symbols
PDesign pressure, psig.
DOutside diameter of the pipe, in. Set by the pipe size.
SAllowable stress at the design temperature, psi (B31.3 Table A-1).
EJoint efficiency: the longitudinal weld quality factor.
YCoefficient, 0.4 for ferritic steel below 900 °F.
tPressure wall: the thinnest wall that contains the pressure, with no allowances.
CACorrosion allowance: wall reserved to be lost to corrosion.
MTMill tolerance: the fraction of nominal wall the mill may ship under.
tmWall with allowances, t + CA. B31.3 calls it the minimum required thickness.
tNOMNominal wall: the schedule’s listed wall, shown in the Nominal wall column.
tLMCAvailable wall in least material condition (LMC): the wall guaranteed in the worst legal case, with the full mill under-run taken and the corrosion allowance used up.
tNOM,minMinimum nominal wall: the thinnest nominal wall that passes. It is the tick on the bars.
Assumptions
Y = 0.4, ferritic materials below 900 °F. Thread and groove depth taken as zero for welded and flanged construction. The weld joint strength reduction factor W is 1 for carbon steel at every temperature this tool allows (B31.3 Table 302.3.5-1), so it does not appear in the formula.
Excludes
Branch reinforcement, external pressure, thermal expansion and support loading, fatigue, Category M and severe cyclic service. Flange, fitting and MDMT requirements are assessed separately.
