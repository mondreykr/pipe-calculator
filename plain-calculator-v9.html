ASME B31.3 — EnerCorp Internal Use

Inputs

Percent removed from nominal wall.

Result

Minimum wall required —
ScheduleWall

Basis
t = PD / 2(SE + PY), ASME B31.3 para. 304.1.2. Minimum wall = (t + corrosion allowance) ÷ (1 − mill tolerance).
Assumptions
Y = 0.4, ferritic materials below 900 °F. Thread and groove depth taken as zero for welded and flanged construction.
Excludes
Branch reinforcement, external pressure, thermal expansion and support loading, fatigue, Category M and severe cyclic service. Flange, fitting and MDMT requirements are assessed separately.
Schedules
EnerCorp permitted set. Sizes 1-1/4, 2-1/2, 3-1/2, 5 and 22 are not used.
Status
Allowable stress values are provisional pending verification against Table A-1. This tool screens only; the line list remains the calculation of record.
